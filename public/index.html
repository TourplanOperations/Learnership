About the learnership

Experience is the first step to a job

One of South Africa's biggest challenges is high unemployment, and graduates often struggle to find work without experience. The travel and tourism industry feels this too, with a shortage of skilled people.

The Tourplan Learnership is our answer. It is open to unemployed graduates who hold a minimum three-year SAQA-recognised diploma or degree in IT, Tourism or Business, ideally, but not only, those who want to work in travel and tourism. It combines technical training, personal development and real-world experience.

What the programme includes

Tourplan software training

Intensive training on Tourplan's proprietary tourism software, with projects and practical assignments.

Soft skills and business English

Workshops on professional, communication and business skills for the world of work.

Practical work experience

Work alongside Tourplan staff on real tasks for clients in the travel industry.

Three phases of three months

  1. 1

    Preparing for the world of work

    March to May. Training on Tourplan's software, plus professional and business skills development.

  2. 2

    Introduction to the world of work

    June to August. Deeper software training and continued professional and business skills development.

  3. 3

    The world of work

    September to November. Working alongside Tourplan staff on day-to-day work.

When you finish

Successful learners receive a Tourplan certificate and a letter of recommendation, with references for their on-the-job practice and learning. Many of our past learners have gone on to jobs in the tourism industry.

How selection works

  1. Apply online with your five supporting documents.
  2. We review every application after the closing date.
  3. Shortlisted applicants complete an in-depth selection process, including psychometric testing.
  4. Selected learners are offered a learnership contract.

Who can apply

  • Unemployed graduates
  • An NQF 7 qualification or higher (a 3-year diploma or degree). NQF 6 will be considered if you excelled.
  • Studied IT, Tourism or Business
  • Final-year students may apply with their latest transcript
  • Able to attend in person in Boksburg, March to November 2027
